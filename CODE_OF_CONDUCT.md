# Code of Conduct

OpenAssist follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep discussions about the work, and do not harass
anyone. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

Report unacceptable behavior by contacting a maintainer directly, or use GitHub's "Report content"
option on the comment or issue. Reports are reviewed confidentially, and maintainers may remove
content or block participants who violate this code.
